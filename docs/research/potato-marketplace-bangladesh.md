# Bangladesh potato marketplace: verified planning notes

Checked 3 October 2026. This is research for product planning, **not** a claim that AluSathi has live market, buyer, storage, logistics, or payment integrations.

## Question, method, and evidence weight

**Question:** For Bangladeshi potato farmers, can an in-app service produce a better *realized net sale* than the local alternative by matching actual cross-district buyer demand and comparing sale-now with storage, without overstating data or regulatory readiness?

This is a **targeted source review, not a systematic review or a farmer study**. Searches and direct checks covered Bangla/English terms for potato/grower/wholesale prices, digital agricultural markets, potato cold storage and post-harvest handling, and payment/escrow rules on DAM, Sadai, AIS, FAO and Bangladesh Bank sites. The linked primary pages below were checked on the date above; older agronomic guidance is used for handling principles, not current prices or store capacity. News summaries, vendor claims, unverified scraped offers and old cold-store counts were excluded. The method follows the [agency-agents Research Synthesist role](https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents/blob/main/research/research-synthesist.md), which calls for claim-to-origin tracing, source grading, explicit gaps and calibrated confidence; the [repository README](https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents/blob/main/README.md) identifies that role as evidence synthesis. That role is a **research method**, not evidence about Bangladeshi markets.

**Overall confidence:** high that these constraints should shape the design; low that AluSathi can yet deliver higher farmer income, because no completed sale, verified buyer order, transport quote, storage vacancy, or before/after farmer outcome has been measured. A missing verified API is an **evidence gap**, not proof that no API exists.

## What the primary sources support

1. **Price reference data exists, but the price level matters.** The Department of Agricultural Marketing (DAM) [commodity-wise report](https://market.dam.gov.bd/commodity_wise_report?L=E) lets a user select division, district, subdistrict, market, commodity, date range, and **Growers / Wholesale / Retail** price type; the page says up to three years can be requested at a time. Its [weekly report](https://market.dam.gov.bd/commodity_wise_weekly_report?L=E) exposes similar filters. A district retail number is not a farm-gate offer; show its type, market, unit, and observation date beside any benchmark. The public site is a report interface; a supported API, licensing terms for automated ingestion, coverage, and refresh reliability have **not** been verified.
2. **A generic online farm-product listing is not novel by itself.** DAM's [Sadai digital market](https://sadai.gov.bd/) describes farmer/business listings and wholesale/retail sales, but its homepage says the platform's features are **still in testing** and that its displayed information, prices, and images are test data (notice dated 21 April 2026). Do not import those listings or prices as real transactions. AluSathi's defensible distinction would be potato-specific, verified buyer offers and *net farmer return after transport, fees, rejection and storage*, not another classifieds page.
3. **Storage is a real alternative to distress selling, but it is not a guaranteed profit strategy.** Bangladesh's Agricultural Information Service (AIS) advises sorting out immature, cut, damaged, infested or diseased tubers, curing harvested potatoes in shade before cold storage, and maintaining ventilation for non-refrigerated storage: [harvest and storage](https://ais.gov.bd/pages/krishi-kotha/%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%B2%E0%A7%81-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%82%E0%A6%97%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%B9-%E0%A6%93-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%82%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B7%E0%A6%A3-7cc21d-6922d941dbfbab28ce04a4fa), [non-refrigerated storage](https://ais.gov.bd/pages/krishi-kotha/%E0%A6%85%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A7%9F%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%A4-%E0%A6%98%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%87-%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%B2%E0%A7%81-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%82%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B7%E0%A6%A3-5f40b7-6922d95adbfbab28ce04baa4). FAO explicitly separates **ware/food potatoes, seed potatoes and processing potatoes**, because their storage goals and temperature ranges differ; ware potatoes need darkness to prevent greening: [FAO storage chapter](https://www.fao.org/4/s1250e/S1250E0y.htm), [FAO root-and-tuber handling](https://www.fao.org/4/X5415E/x5415e04.htm). A household **freezer is the wrong default suggestion**: freezing damages potato tissue, as FAO's [food-processing guide](https://www.fao.org/4/a1549e/a1549e01.pdf) warns. Recommend a suitable potato **cold store** or ventilated short-term storage, chosen by potato purpose and local expert/provider advice—not a universal temperature or a freezer.
4. **Money handling is a separate regulated product.** Bangladesh Bank says domestic MFS supports P2P, P2B and B2P payments, while PSP/PSO activities require licensing: [payment systems](https://www.bb.org.bd/en/index.php/financialsystems/paysystems). Its [MFS Regulations 2022](https://www.bb.org.bd/aboutus/regulationguideline/psd/mfs_regulations_2022.pdf) and [2025 Payment Systems Report](https://www.bb.org.bd/pub/annual/psdreport/paymentreport_dec2025.pdf) also identify a 2023 merchant-acquiring/escrow guideline. MVP should record an agreed payment method and receipt/reference **without receiving or holding money**. Integrated checkout, deposits or escrow require a licensed provider and legal/compliance review; do not call an unlicensed in-app balance “escrow.”

## Product implications (recommendations, not claims about existing services)

- **Offer comparison, not “highest price wins”:** capture lot size, variety, food/seed/processing use, farmer-declared quality, grade/size mix, district, pickup date, farmgate vs delivered price, payment timing, rejection/weight-deduction terms, buyer pickup or transport quote. Estimate `accepted kg × agreed price − transport − loading − market fees − storage/handling − return or disposal cost for rejected kg`; show the assumptions, unsold/rejected quantity and a range, not a promised profit. Compare like quantities or account for the value of any remainder rather than hiding it in a per-kg figure.
- **Real buyer demand:** onboard a small number of identifiable traders, wholesalers, processors and institutional purchasers; require them to post quantity, variety/grade, delivery area, date window, offered price or bid range, payment terms, and offer expiry. Show verification level and last activity. Do not invent “live buyers.”
- **Farmer-controlled deal record:** one-tap call/message, a simple Bangla offer summary, acceptance/collection date, weighbridge or scale photo, signed/OTP-confirmed weight and grade, payment receipt, and a dispute trail. A buyer's claim that potatoes are “bad” should not silently change the agreed price.
- **Storage comparison:** a directory can start with manually verified stores and phone numbers. Show availability as “call to confirm” until partners report capacity; request itemized quotes for bag/tonnage basis, duration, loading/unloading, transport, withdrawal fees, insurance or loss rules, operating hours and suitability for **food versus seed** potatoes. Calculate a break-even future sale price after costs and weight loss. No public realtime capacity or booking API was verified.
- **Low-literacy interface:** farmer home screen should have only “Sell my potatoes”, “See buyer offers”, and “Storage/transport”; Bangla first, voice-assisted entry optional, quantities and money always read back before posting, no dense trading dashboard.
- **Trust and impact measurement:** pilot one sourcing district and two destination markets, log real completed sales, compare the farmer's **total net proceeds for the same original lot** with a documented local alternative, and track any unsold remainder, repeat buyers, failed pickups, rejected lots, days to payment, and disputes. Do not claim higher income from posted asking prices alone.

## Integration status to verify before implementation

| Source/service | Observed public surface | Not yet demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DAM | Filterable grower/wholesale/retail reports and market directory | Supported API, permitted automated reuse, complete potato history, guaranteed freshness |
| Sadai | Listing concept with explicit testing notice | Production-quality live offers or transactions |
| Cold storage | AIS/FAO handling guidance; potential manually built partner directory | Live vacancy, bookable capacity, standardized tariffs |
| Payments | Bangladesh Bank-regulated MFS/PSP/PSO ecosystem | AluSathi payment or escrow authorization/integration |

Before committing to automation, contact DAM and pilot storage/buyer partners for data access and operational terms. The first honest version can still complete an in-app match and deal record while the actual payment is made through an existing licensed channel.

## Minimum field validation before promising a marketplace

1. **Demand:** Which named buyers in which destination districts will sign a dated purchase request for specific variety, grade, minimum/maximum quantity, pickup window and price? How often do they buy, and what quality evidence will they accept before dispatch?
2. **Counterfactual:** On the *same day and grade*, what local farm-gate offer could the farmer actually have accepted? Record it, not a retail quote or an average from a different date.
3. **Execution:** Who pays for sacks, loading, truck, unloading and any market fees; whose scale determines accepted kilograms; what happens to rejected potatoes; who bears late pickup or non-payment risk?
4. **Storage:** Can a named cold store confirm capacity, suitable conditions for this lot's food/seed/processing purpose, all-in charges, deposit, withdrawal rules and weight-loss policy? At what future price would storage beat today's best confirmed sale **after** those costs and likely loss?
5. **Data and payments:** Will DAM authorize reusable, timely grower/wholesale data, and by what route? Which licensed payment provider can handle checkout or protected settlement if needed? Until both are confirmed, use labeled manual reference prices and external licensed payment.
6. **Pilot success:** For each completed deal, compare realized **total net taka on the same original lot**, including any unsold or rejected potatoes, and days-to-payment against that farmer's documented alternative; also log failed pickups, grade disputes and repeat purchases. A posted high bid alone is not evidence of impact.
