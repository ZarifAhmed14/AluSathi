## Source strength

Company pages are primary evidence for what a company publicly posts, but their service-quality statements are promotional and not independent reliability proof. EPB is primary evidence of the published government registry entry, not an endorsement or confirmation of operating capacity. Contact/source confidence is stronger than service-availability confidence.
